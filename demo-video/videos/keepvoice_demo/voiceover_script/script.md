# keepvoice demo

## kv-problem
You recorded a video. But a song is playing in the background, and that song is copyrighted.

## kv-vocals
A normal vocal remover does not fix this. It keeps every human voice, so the singers stay in your track.

## kv-model
keepvoice uses a model trained on film sound. It splits the audio into speech, music, and effects, and keeps only the speech.

## kv-result
On a real restaurant video, the song fell from minus thirty-nine decibels, to below minus eighty-one. The picture is copied, bit for bit.

## kv-cta
One command. Open source. keepvoice.
